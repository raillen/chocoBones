# Wireframe Fidelity Levels

When designing wireframes, it is crucial to choose the right level of fidelity based on the project phase and the audience.

## Low-Fidelity (Lo-Fi)
- **Visuals:** Rough sketches, gray boxes, placeholder text (lorem ipsum).
- **Goal:** Rapid ideation, determining layout, establishing visual hierarchy, and basic user flow.
- **Tools:** Pen and paper, whiteboards, Balsamiq, simple HTML/CSS blocking.
- **Audience:** Internal design team, product managers, early stakeholder alignment.

## Medium-Fidelity (Mid-Fi)
- **Visuals:** Greyscale, precise spacing, real or near-real text, standard UI components (buttons, inputs) without styling.
- **Goal:** Evaluating usability, defining interactions, finalizing content structure.
- **Tools:** Figma, Sketch, Adobe XD, generic UI kits.
- **Audience:** Development team (for early technical scoping), usability testing participants.

## High-Fidelity (Hi-Fi)
- **Visuals:** Full color, typography, imagery, interactions, animations. Looks exactly like the final product.
- **Goal:** Final design approval, detailed developer handoff, final usability validation.
- **Tools:** Figma, React/HTML prototypes.
- **Audience:** Clients, executive stakeholders, engineering team (for implementation).
